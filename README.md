# InstaPaste

> **A privacy-first iOS app for saving, organizing, and instantly pasting reusable text templates across any app.**

InstaPaste helps eliminate repetitive typing by allowing users to create reusable text snippets and access them instantly through a custom iOS keyboard. Whether you're replying to customers, sending emails, writing social media captions, or managing frequently used messages, InstaPaste keeps everything organized, accessible, and completely private.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📝 Create and manage reusable text templates
- 🏷️ Organize templates using tags
- ⭐ Mark frequently used templates as favorites
- 🔍 Instantly search templates
- 📋 Switch between list and grid views
- ↕️ Sort templates for quick access
- ⌨️ Custom iOS keyboard for cross-app pasting
- 📈 Smart prioritization using Recent and Frequently Used templates
- 📦 Import and export templates in JSON format
- 📱 Fully offline with on-device storage
- 🔒 No tracking, analytics, or cloud dependency

---

## 🚀 Why InstaPaste?

We all copy and paste the same messages repeatedly—customer responses, email signatures, meeting links, social media captions, addresses, notes, and much more.

InstaPaste simplifies this workflow by providing a fast, organized, and privacy-focused solution that lets you reuse text anywhere on your iPhone.

Unlike many productivity apps, InstaPaste keeps your data entirely on your device. No accounts, no servers, and no internet connection are required.

## ⌨️ Custom Keyboard Extension

One of InstaPaste's most powerful features is its custom iOS keyboard.

Without leaving your current application, you can:

- Browse saved templates
- View favorite templates
- Search for snippets
- Tap once to paste instantly
- Reuse text across virtually any app

This makes replying, messaging, emailing, and documenting significantly faster.

## 🎯 Design Philosophy

InstaPaste is built around three simple principles:

- **Fast** – Access and paste text in seconds.
- **Simple** – Clean, intuitive interface with minimal friction.
- **Private** – Everything stays on your device.

Every feature is designed to reduce repetitive typing while maintaining a smooth and native iOS experience.
